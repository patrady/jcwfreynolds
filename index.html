About Reynolds Oil Company

For 100 years, Reynolds Oil Company has been a trusted name in the North Texas oil and gas industry. What began as a small operation during the historic Burkburnett oil boom has grown into a respected family enterprise, now in its fourth generation of leadership.

Headquartered in Wichita Falls, Texas, we remain committed to the values that built this company: integrity, hard work, and a deep respect for the land and communities we serve.

Our History

1918

The Burkburnett Boom

Oil was discovered in Burkburnett, Texas, sparking one of the most dramatic oil booms in American history. The small farming town transformed overnight into "Boomtown USA," attracting thousands of wildcatters, roughnecks, and entrepreneurs seeking their fortune.

1926

Reynolds Oil Founded

James Walter Reynolds, having worked the Burkburnett fields for nearly a decade, established Reynolds Oil Company in nearby Wichita Falls. With hard-earned experience and a reputation for honest dealing, he built the foundation for what would become a family legacy.

1950s

Second Generation

James Jr. took the reins, expanding operations throughout North Texas and Southern Oklahoma. The company weathered market fluctuations by maintaining conservative practices and investing in emerging technologies.

1980s

Third Generation

The Reynolds grandchildren modernized operations while honoring the company's traditional values. Diversification into oil field services helped stabilize the business during the volatile decade.

Today

Fourth Generation

Now led by the fourth generation, Reynolds Oil Company continues to operate with the same principles established a century ago. We proudly maintain our headquarters in Wichita Falls, contributing to the community that has supported our family for generations.
