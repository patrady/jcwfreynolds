About J.C. & W.F. Reynolds Oil Producers

For over a century, J.C. & W.F. Reynolds Oil Producers has been part of the North Texas oil and gas industry. Founded during the historic Burkburnett oil boom of 1918, the company has grown into a respected family enterprise, now in its third generation.

We remain committed to the values that built this company: integrity, hard work, and a deep respect for the land.

Our History

1918

The Burkburnett Boom

Oil was discovered in Burkburnett, Texas, sparking one of the most dramatic oil booms in American history. The small farming town transformed overnight into "Boomtown USA." J.C. and W.F. Reynolds seized the opportunity, establishing J.C. & W.F. Reynolds Oil Producers during this historic period.

1950s

Second Generation

The second generation took the reins, expanding operations throughout North Texas. The company weathered market fluctuations by maintaining conservative practices and investing in emerging technologies.

Today

Third Generation

Now led by the third generation, J.C. & W.F. Reynolds Oil Producers continues to operate with the same principles established over a century ago.
